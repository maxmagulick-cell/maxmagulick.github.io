// 01 — About

ABOUT ME

I spent a year and a half as an Account Manager at IntermixIT, a managed service provider, where I coordinated support for roughly 70 client accounts. My day-to-day was client communication, service request tracking in Autotask, documentation, and working alongside the engineers who handled escalations.

That role taught me how IT service delivery actually works — how tickets move, how clients describe problems versus what's really happening, and how much of good support is organization and follow-through. I also worked in Microsoft 365 every day, which is what pushed me to get MS-900 certified.

Outside of work I build automation projects with Zapier, n8n, Python, Azure AI, and Streamlit. These are learning projects, not enterprise software — I build them because I like understanding how systems connect, and each one has taught me something I couldn't get from a course alone.

I passed CompTIA A+ Core 1 in July 2026 and I'm currently studying for Core 2. I'm looking for a help desk, desktop support, or IT support role where I can build real technical depth.
